SEO_Next

SEO_Next is an internal SEO data analytics platform developed and operated by Ged Group S.r.l. It aggregates data from Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, and Google Ads Keyword Planner to support SEO strategy, reporting, and keyword research for managed web properties.

Access to the platform is restricted to authorized Ged Group personnel. This page exists solely to provide public information about the application, as required by Google API policies.
