Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SEO_Next is an internal application developed and operated by Ged Group S.r.l. ("we", "us"). This policy describes how we handle data in connection with the application's use of Google APIs.

Data we access

With explicit authorization (OAuth 2.0) from the connected Google account, SEO_Next accesses:

  • Google Search Console data (search performance metrics for authorized properties)
  • Google Analytics 4 data (traffic and conversion metrics for authorized properties)
  • Google Ads Keyword Planner data (keyword search volume and competition metrics)

How data is used

Data is used exclusively for internal SEO analysis, reporting, and strategy purposes related to the properties for which access was granted. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with unaffiliated third parties.

How data is stored

OAuth credentials and access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SEO_Next's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke SEO_Next's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account permissions.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact form on our homepage.
